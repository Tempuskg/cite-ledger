# Publication log

Every item an agent publishes for Cite Ledger is logged here, on the day it is published. That covers articles, changelog entries, README edits and Discussions replies. Darren reviews this log weekly.

Publishing happens only after launch, only under the accepted marketing standing instruction, and only within its channels, templates (T1–T5), volume limits and claim register. Before each item, the agent confirms that the instruction is in force and not past its expiry. Items outside the instruction go to Darren for approval, and the "Authority" column records his approval.

Volume limits:
- at most 1 article a week and 4 a calendar month;
- 1 changelog entry per approved release;
- at most 10 Discussions replies a week, and at most 2 per thread before handing the thread to Darren;
- nothing published between 21:00 and 06:00 America/Regina.
